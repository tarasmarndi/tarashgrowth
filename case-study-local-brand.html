Local brand awareness campaign

Planning a neighbourhood coffee-shop campaign that connects local reach, an opening-day incentive and a reason to return.

Anonymised projectMeta AdsStrategy & execution
Confidentiality note: This case study intentionally omits client identity, account screenshots, customer information and non-public performance data. Replace prompts with details you are authorised to share. Do not publish illustrative figures as actual results.

01. Project overview

The project involved campaign planning for a local coffee business promoting a store opening. The offer was designed to introduce nearby customers to the business and encourage a second visit through a follow-up coffee benefit.

  • Industry: Local consumer business
  • Objective: Increase local awareness and encourage a measurable customer action
  • My contribution: Local audience planning, messaging direction, offer communication and campaign structure

02. The challenge

A local promotion needs more than broad awareness. People should understand where the business is, what the offer includes, when it is valid and what action to take next.

03. Research & planning

  • Considered the business location and the practical nearby audience.
  • Reviewed the offer conditions, including the qualifying bill value and redemption period.
  • Identified the key information that needed to appear consistently across ad copy and creative.

04. Strategy & execution

  • Planned location-focused audience targeting around the store's catchment area.
  • Structured the message around the opening-day coupon and the next-visit benefit.
  • Prioritised clear offer terms, validity and call to action in the creative direction.
  • Matched the awareness objective to the goal of introducing the local business to potential customers.

05. Measurement & learning

For an awareness campaign, review reach, frequency, CPM and local engagement. Where tracking is available, assess directions, messages, offer redemptions or store visits separately. Awareness metrics alone should not be presented as proof of sales impact.

Results: The case study documents the campaign planning approach. Business performance and redemption figures are not published here.

06. My key takeaways

Local campaigns work best when location, offer clarity and customer action reinforce one another. The offer should be easy to understand before someone decides to visit.
