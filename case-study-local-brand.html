Local brand awareness campaign

An anonymised example of local audience planning, offer-led messaging and campaign objective selection for a location-based business.

Anonymised projectMeta AdsStrategy & execution
Confidentiality note: This case study intentionally omits client identity, account screenshots, customer information and non-public performance data. Replace prompts with details you are authorised to share. Do not publish illustrative figures as actual results.

01. Project overview

The project involved planning a local campaign designed to introduce a business to nearby potential customers and communicate a time-bound offer.

  • Industry: Local consumer business
  • Objective: Increase local awareness and encourage a measurable customer action
  • My contribution: Local audience planning, messaging direction, offer communication and campaign structure

02. The challenge

The campaign needed to reach a relevant geographic audience while making the offer easy to understand and act on.

03. Research & planning

  • Define the practical service area around the business location.
  • Identify likely customer motivations and objections.
  • Check that offer conditions, dates and redemption steps are clear.

04. Strategy & execution

  • Use location-relevant messaging and creative.
  • Make the offer, eligibility and call to action prominent.
  • Match campaign objective and optimisation event to the intended outcome.

05. Measurement & learning

Review reach and frequency for awareness, then monitor available downstream actions such as directions, messages, landing-page visits or tracked conversions. Select metrics based on the campaign objective.

Results: Add only approved, verifiable results. If client metrics are confidential, state: “Campaign metrics withheld.” If the campaign was only planned, label it as a planning example.

06. My key takeaways

For local campaigns, geographic relevance and offer clarity should work together. A broad reach number is not enough to judge business impact.
