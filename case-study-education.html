Education lead generation strategy

Planning a segmented Meta Ads approach for school admissions, with messaging shaped around different learning stages and parent priorities.

Anonymised projectMeta AdsStrategy & execution
Confidentiality note: This case study intentionally omits client identity, account screenshots, customer information and non-public performance data. Replace prompts with details you are authorised to share. Do not publish illustrative figures as actual results.

01. Project overview

The work centred on an admissions campaign for an educational institution expanding its presence with a new school location. The communication needed to speak to families considering different entry points, from early learning through middle school.

  • Industry: Education
  • Objective: Generate relevant admissions enquiries and build local awareness
  • My contribution: Audience research, segmentation approach, creative strategy and campaign planning

02. The challenge

A single generic admissions message would not address the different questions parents ask at each stage. The campaign needed a clear structure that connected audience needs, creative messaging and the enquiry journey.

03. Research & planning

  • Separated the audience into pre-primary, primary and middle-school groups.
  • Considered parent priorities and questions at each learning stage.
  • Reviewed programme information and website content to identify relevant proof points and messaging themes.

04. Strategy & execution

  • Developed distinct creative directions for each class group rather than repeating one message across all audiences.
  • Mapped concepts to awareness, problem-aware and solution-aware communication needs.
  • Planned lead-generation messaging and form considerations to make the enquiry step clear.
  • Organised the campaign approach around audience relevance, creative variation and a measurable enquiry objective.

05. Measurement & learning

A live campaign should be evaluated using delivery and lead-quality indicators together: reach, frequency, CTR, form completion rate, cost per lead and the relevance of enquiries. These metrics should be interpreted against the campaign objective and available CRM feedback.

Results: This portfolio summary presents the strategy and planning work. Campaign performance figures are not included because verified, approved metrics are not available for public disclosure.

06. My key takeaways

Segmentation is valuable when it changes the message, creative and user journey for each audience. Dividing ad sets alone does not create a meaningful strategy.
