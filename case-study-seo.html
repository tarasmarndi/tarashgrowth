SEO research & optimisation

A practical framework for turning keyword research and search intent into prioritised on-page SEO recommendations.

01. Project overview

This case study demonstrates a structured SEO workflow for understanding search demand and identifying page-level improvement opportunities.

  • Industry: Website / service business
  • Objective: Improve relevance between target search queries and useful website content
  • My contribution: Keyword research, intent mapping, page review and optimisation recommendations

02. The challenge

Websites can target broad keywords without fully addressing what searchers need. The task is to identify the intent behind queries and map it to the most relevant pages.

03. Research & planning

  • Collect seed terms and expand them into relevant keyword groups.
  • Classify search intent and identify the page type that best satisfies it.
  • Review existing pages, titles, headings, content coverage and internal links.

04. Strategy & execution

  • Cluster related keywords by topic and intent.
  • Map keyword groups to existing or proposed landing pages.
  • Prioritise title, heading, content and internal-link improvements.
  • Document technical issues for further validation.

05. Measurement & learning

Track organic clicks, impressions, CTR, average position and relevant conversions over a suitable period. Compare like-for-like pages and query groups where possible.

Results: Insert verified Search Console or analytics outcomes only if you have permission. Otherwise state: “Performance figures not disclosed” or describe the deliverables completed.

06. My key takeaways

Keyword volume is only one input. Search intent, page usefulness and the ability to measure meaningful outcomes should guide prioritisation.
